Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to the most common questions.

What is your pricing model?

We offer tiered monthly and annual plans. Annual subscriptions include a discount. Custom enterprise pricing is available for larger teams or additional compliance needs.

Do you provide a free trial?

Yes. New accounts receive a 14-day free trial with full access to core features. No credit card is required to start.

How do invoices and billing work?

We support credit card and bank transfer for eligible plans. Invoices are generated automatically and can be downloaded from the billing portal. VAT details can be added to your company profile.

Can we cancel anytime? What is your refund policy?

You can cancel at any time from the admin panel. Plans remain active until the end of the billing cycle. We offer prorated refunds for annual plans within 30 days of purchase.

How do you secure our data?

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256). Access is role-based with audit logs. We run regular penetration tests and continuous vulnerability scanning.

Are you compliant with GDPR and SOC 2?

Yes. We are GDPR-aligned and maintain SOC 2 Type II controls. Data Processing Agreements (DPA) and Sub-processor lists are available upon request.

What uptime do you guarantee? Do you offer an SLA?

We target 99.9% uptime. Enterprise plans include a formal SLA with response and resolution targets, plus priority support channels.

Do you have SSO and role-based access control?

Yes. We support SSO via SAML/OIDC and granular RBAC with predefined roles and custom permissions for teams and projects.

Is there an API and what integrations are available?

We provide a REST API and webhooks. Native integrations include Slack, Microsoft Teams, GitHub, GitLab, Jira, and Zapier. Enterprise customers can request custom connectors.

How do you handle backups and disaster recovery?

Automated encrypted backups run multiple times per day with multi-region redundancy. We regularly test restore procedures to meet RPO/RTO objectives.

Can we export or delete our data?

Yes. Admins can export data in open formats via the product or API. Upon contract termination, data can be deleted on request following our retention policy.

What onboarding and support do you provide?

All plans include help center, email support, and product tours. Business/Enterprise add live training, dedicated success, and migration assistance.

What are your support hours and response times?

Standard support is 9:00–18:00 (GMT+3) on business days. Priority and 24/7 options are included in Enterprise SLAs with defined response targets by severity.
